The first website I chose was clickclickclickclick. This website is special because while many site attempt to gameify the simple act of clicking a button, this site tracks elements of user input that most games would not. The site is centered around achievements, many of which have to do with the frequency at which the player clicks the button--however, many of the achievements have to do with the way the user moves their cursor around the screen, or if they click something that is not the button. This, to me, is unique.

The second website I chose was Emoji Simulator. This one was really neat because it features interactable emojis (in my case, trees, although it is easily changeable) that I could light on fire and see them disappear. What made it more interesting to me was my ability to change the spawn rate of the trees, allowing me to rapidly grow trees so I could more easily burn down the whole "forest". My favorite part was that once I changed the tree spawn rate to 100%, I could make fun patterns with the fire.

The third website I chose was Pokémon Explorer. As a competitive Pokémon player I was immediately intrigued by this--a collection of Pokémon, able to be filtered by specific stat numbers to find exactly what you are looking for. One thing I noticed, however, is that this collection is not up to date, and is missing any Pokémon added after the release of Scarlet & Violet.
